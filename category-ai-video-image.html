Creative production

Creative AI should improve the asset, not just make more stuff.

AI video and image tools are valuable when they shorten iteration cycles, create usable variants, or unlock production that would otherwise be too slow or expensive.

How to evaluate creative AI tools

Creative AI tools should be judged by output quality, editability, rights clarity, brand consistency, speed, and whether a non-designer can reach a usable result without endless regeneration.

The best creative AI workflow is rarely one prompt. It is prompt, edit, constrain, remix, and publish.
