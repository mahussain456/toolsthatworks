About Tools That Work

Tools That Work is an independent publication covering AI and SaaS tools for founders, operators, and solopreneurs. The goal is simple: test tools in real workflows, publish clear verdicts, and avoid vendor hype.

How we review

We prioritize hands-on testing, direct subscriptions, practical workflows, and honest tradeoffs. If a tool is not worth the money, the review should say so plainly.

Editorial stance

We do not publish paid placements disguised as reviews. If a page contains affiliate links, that relationship is disclosed clearly. The presence of a commission opportunity does not change the verdict.
